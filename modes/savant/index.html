The Neuroscience

The alternative has been hiding in plain sight

Dyslexic brains have more long-range connections between distant neural regions and fewer short-range connections between adjacent ones. The short-range reduction creates reading difficulty. The long-range surplus creates cross-domain pattern recognition at a speed and scale sequential cognition cannot match.

Cambridge researcher Helen Taylor called it Complementary Cognition (2022). Two styles, evolved together: exploitative minds that process known information, and explorative minds that discover new patterns in unknown territory. Both essential. One rewarded by every institution humans have built. The other called a disability.

Oracle — How you choose

An AI that helps you think is one thing. An AI that helps you choose is another thing entirely. Seven principles grounded in Kahneman, Klein, and Bayesian cognition. Fully designed. Not yet shipped.

Research Direction III

Quest — The meaning engine

Vervaeke diagnosed the meaning crisis: we lost the frameworks that provided meaning and replaced them with nothing. Quest is a direct response. Not gamification. A structure for meaning.

Your ventures are realms. Your sessions are quests. A Virtue Compass measures not how far you walked but how you walked. Campbell's monomyth. Frankl's will to meaning. The Grail Quest tradition.

Panksepp's SEEKING system fires during pursuit, not arrival. Quest focuses on departure — the dopamine hit comes when you set out, not when you finish.

Agglutinative languages encode relationship, temporality, and intentionality within single morphological units. One word contains what English needs an entire clause to express. Hungarian. Estonian. Turkish. Japanese.

Neural networks build meaning through layer composition. These are structurally homologous. The morphological layering of an agglutinative word mirrors the layer composition of a neural network. The language of the document is the programming language.

The first experiment: write the Savant Mode specification natively in Hungarian. Run identical prompts through both. Measure the outputs. Academic partner validates. Results published open source.
